# Decision Models

Open this file for option selection, tradeoffs, prioritization, and judgment quality.

| Model | Use when | Ask | Pairs well with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| expected value and probabilistic thinking | outcomes are uncertain but estimable | what is the weighted value across plausible outcomes? | ergodicity, opportunity cost |
| expected utility and prospect theory | behavior depends on framing, loss aversion, or distorted probabilities | how will real humans perceive gains, losses, and reference points? | bounded rationality, bias checklist |
| inversion | failure is easier to see than success | what would reliably cause this to fail, and how do we avoid it? | black swan, pre-mortem style analysis |
| opportunity cost | resources are scarce and alternatives matter | what is the best thing we give up by choosing this? | marginal thinking, second-order thinking |
| marginal thinking | the next unit matters more than the average | what is the incremental cost and incremental benefit of doing more? | opportunity cost, diminishing returns |
| second-order thinking | direct effects look good but reactions may dominate | what happens after the first obvious consequence? | game theory, path dependence |
| bounded rationality | the plan assumes perfect information or unlimited attention | what would a satisficing human actually do here? | prospect theory, incentives |
| cognitive bias checklist | confidence outruns evidence quality | which bias is most likely distorting this judgment? | falsifiability, Dunning-Kruger and circle of competence |
| Dunning-Kruger and circle of competence | expertise is thin or hidden ignorance is likely | what do we know well, and where are we pretending? | information theory, latticework of mental models |

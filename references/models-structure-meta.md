# Structure And Meta Models

Open this file when the frame itself may be wrong, when patterns recur across scales, or when you need synthesis across conflicting views.

| Model | Use when | Ask | Pairs well with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| paradigm shift | current facts fit badly because the frame itself is stale | what changes if we swap the governing worldview? | dialectics, latticework of mental models |
| symmetry and conservation | invariants constrain what can change | what stays constant across transformations or scenarios? | first principles, scaling laws |
| recursion and self-similarity | the same pattern repeats across levels | what structure shows up again at smaller or larger scales? | emergence, path dependence |
| latticework of mental models | one discipline is dominating the answer | which other fields offer a better lens on this problem? | paradigm shift, circle of competence |
| dialectics | two opposing views both contain real signal | what synthesis becomes visible if we take both sides seriously? | paradigm shift, second-order thinking |

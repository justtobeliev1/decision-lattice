# Systems Models

Open this file for coupled systems, interventions, lock-in, flywheels, and operational drift.

| Model | Use when | Ask | Pairs well with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| feedback loops | the problem changes as people react to it | what reinforces, what stabilizes, and what delays matter? | leverage points, path dependence |
| emergence | local behavior creates surprising macro outcomes | what property appears only at the whole-system level? | feedback loops, network effects |
| network effects and power laws | winners concentrate, popularity compounds, or the tail matters | does value rise with participation, and is the distribution heavy-tailed? | compounding, game theory |
| entropy and thermodynamics | order decays without maintenance or energy input | what drift, decay, or coordination cost will appear if we stop investing? | leverage points, antifragility |
| path dependence | early moves or accidents create lock-in | what small early conditions keep shaping the present? | feedback loops, leverage points |
| leverage points | small interventions may move the whole system | where can a small change alter information flows, rules, or goals? | feedback loops, entropy |

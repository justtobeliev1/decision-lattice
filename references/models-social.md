# Social Models

Open this file for competition, coordination, org design, institutions, and hidden motives.

| Model | Use when | Ask | Pairs well with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| natural selection | many variants compete and only some persist | what variation, selection pressure, and inheritance mechanism exist? | antifragility, game theory |
| game theory | outcomes depend on what others choose | what strategy profile is stable given mutual adaptation? | incentives, comparative advantage |
| incentives | stated goals and actual behavior diverge | who benefits, who pays, and what behavior is rewarded? | game theory, agency analysis |
| comparative advantage | specialization and exchange could unlock gains | who should do what given relative strengths and tradeoffs? | incentives, marginal thinking |
| social construction and collective imagination | institutions or norms drive behavior even without physical necessity | what shared story or legitimacy structure is doing the work? | game theory, paradigm shift |
| unconscious motives and defense mechanisms | behavior seems inconsistent with explicit reasoning | what hidden motive, identity defense, or self-protection may be active? | biases, bounded rationality |
